ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology That Connects People

SurplusToShelter is a food rescue platform designed to connect people and organizations that have surplus food with shelters and communities that can use it.

Instead of allowing usable food to become waste, the platform creates a simple digital flow from donation to delivery.

✓ Easy donation process
✓ Smart recipient matching
✓ Volunteer pickup coordination
✓ Transparent impact tracking
